1.0.0

  • Initial release, based on AutoAnswer9 by leftyfl1p (rewritten).
  • Automatically answers incoming calls after a delay (0–40 s, default 10) for Phone, FaceTime and CallKit apps: Zalo, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Viber, Signal, LINE.
  • Per-app mode: Off (default), Everyone (with excluded contacts) or Only Specific Contacts; match by phone number, caller name or pick from Contacts.
  • Optionally switch auto-answered calls to the speaker, keeping headphones if connected.
  • FaceTime video calls: don't answer, answer as audio only, or answer.
  • Settings apply immediately, no respring needed.
