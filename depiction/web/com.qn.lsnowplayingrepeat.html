Compatibility: This package is compatible with iOS iOS 16.0 to 17.7.

Lock Screen and Control Center Now Playing get a Repeat button for Apple Music, YouTube Music and Spotify (tap = Off/All/One). iOS 17.1+: replaces Favorite, long-press = Favorite. iOS 16–17.0: replaces the audio output button, long-press = output picker.

1.3.0

  • iOS 16 support: on iOS 16.0–17.0.x the Repeat button replaces the audio output button, and long-press opens the output picker.
  • iOS 17.1+ unchanged: replaces Favorite, long-press toggles Favorite.

1.2.0

  • Experimental Spotify support.

1.1.0

  • Repeat button now also works in Control Center's Now Playing module.
  • StandBy and the Dynamic Island keep the stock Favorite button.
  • Respring after installing or removing so Control Center picks up the change.

1.0.0

  • Initial release.
  • Turns the Favorite (⭐) button in the Lock Screen and Control Center Now Playing controls into a Repeat button: tap to cycle Off → All → One, long-press to toggle Favorite.
  • Works with Apple Music and YouTube Music.
