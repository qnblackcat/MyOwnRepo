2.1.0

  • Roothide build.
  • Two-line display: the full expression (e.g. 2×(1+3)) sits above the result, with long expressions scrolling to keep what you're typing visible.
  • Proper order of operations with parentheses; 2( means 2×( and 200+10% means 10% of 200, like the stock Calculator.
  • Tapping = again repeats the last operation.
  • New result history: tap an entry to copy it.
  • Taller module with an extra row of buttons; very large or small results switch to scientific notation.
